## Full license texts / Vollständige Lizenztexte

- **In the app / In der App:** Settings → About PoeziCode → Third-party licenses
  (Einstellungen → Über PoeziCode → Lizenzen von Drittanbietern).
- **Debian/Ubuntu package:** `/usr/share/doc/poezicode/`
- **Arch package:** `/usr/share/licenses/poezicode/`
- **Windows (installer and ZIP / Installer und ZIP):** `licenses\` in the program folder /
  im Programmordner.
- **All packages / Alle Pakete:** `data/flutter_assets/NOTICES.Z` (Flutter and all Dart
  packages), `data/flutter_assets/assets/licenses/pdfium-LICENSE.txt` (PDFium) and
  `data/flutter_assets/assets/fonts/DejaVu-LICENSE.txt` (DejaVu fonts).
